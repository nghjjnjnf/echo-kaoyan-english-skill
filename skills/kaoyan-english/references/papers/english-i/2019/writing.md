# 2019 考研英语一 Section III Writing

Section III Writing
Part A
51. Directions:
Suppose you are working for the “Aiding Rural Primary Schools” project
of your university. Write an email to answer the inquiry from an international
student volunteer, specifying the details of the project.
You should write about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET.
Do not use your own name in the email; use “Li Ming” instead. (10 points)
Part B
52. Directions:
Write an essay of 160-200 words based on the picture below. In your essay,
you should
1) describe the picture briefly,
2) interpret the implied meaning, and
3) give your comments.
Write your answer on the ANSWER SHEET. (20 points)
